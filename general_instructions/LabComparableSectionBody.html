In this section you will complete three blocks of 10 periods.

You are the manager of a firm. Your task is to decide when and how many units of your firm’s equipment to retire.

At the beginning of each block, your firm has 100 units of equipment.

Retiring equipment has a cost that is deducted from your budget.

Your payment depends on the points in your budget at the end of the externally selected block.

Both your firm’s equipment and your budget are always visible on screen.

At the beginning of every block, your firm’s equipment resets to 100 units and your budget to 1000 points. They do not carry over from one block to the next one.

Your Decision

In each period, you choose how many units of your firm’s equipment to retire.

This amount can be any whole number from 0 up to the amount of equipment currently available.

You can enter this amount using the slider. When you are ready to record your decision, click “Confirm”.

Any equipment of your firm that you retire cannot be restored within that block.

The number of units in your firm’s equipment can stay the same or decrease, but it cannot increase during a block.

Once your firm’s equipment reaches 0, it stays at 0 for the rest of the block:

  • You cannot retire any further units.
  • Use Continue to submit retirement of 0 and wait for the other members of your group.
  • You continue through the remaining periods of the block and can observe the information shown on screen.

Your budget may become negative and does not restrict the amount of equipment you may retire.

Main Check

{{ if is_fixed }}

An external authority evaluates the number of units in your firm’s equipment.

Main Check

After your decision in Period 5, the authority checks the number of units in your firm’s equipment.

  • If your firm’s equipment is 0 (i.e., all units have been retired by Period 5), no penalty is applied.
  • If your firm’s equipment is greater than 0 (i.e., some units remain by Period 5), a penalty is deducted from your budget. The penalty is 175 points + 0.5 points for each unit of your firm's equipment.

    • Therefore, the Main Check penalty ranges from 175.5 points (when the main check applies to 1 unit of equipment in your firm's equipment) to 225 points (when the main check applies to 100 units).

Examples:

1) At the end of Period 5, your firm’s equipment is 0 units, and 700 points are in your budget. No penalty is applied. Your budget therefore remains at 700 points.

2) At the end of Period 5, your firm’s equipment is 50 units, and 700 points are in your budget.

The penalty is 175 + 0.5 × 50 = 200 points.

After the deduction, your budget contains 500 points:

700 points − 200 points penalty = 500 points.

The block then continues to Period 6.

The group average is calculated using your firm’s equipment and the equipment of the other five firms in your group. In Section 1, group average equipment is shown for information only. It does not determine your Main fine; there is no group threshold in this section.

{{ elif is_ambiguity }}

For each block, a new threshold is determined.

The threshold can be different in every block of Section 2.

The threshold is the value compared with the group average equipment to determine whether the Main Check penalty applies.

The threshold can be any whole number from 33 to 47 units, inclusive. Each of these values is possible, but you are not given the probabilities of the different values.

You do not know which threshold has been selected until the Main Check at the end of Period 5, after all six participants have completed their decisions.

At the end of Period 5, when all six managers have completed their decisions, the authority compares the group average equipment with the threshold for that block. Your firm’s equipment is included in this average.

First, the authority checks your firm’s equipment:

  • If your firm’s equipment is 0 (i.e., all units have been retired by Period 5), no penalty is applied.

  • If your firm’s equipment is greater than 0 (i.e., some units remain), whether a penalty is applied depends on the group average equipment.

    • If the group average equipment is greater than the threshold for that block, no penalty is applied.

    • If the group average equipment is equal to or less than the threshold for that block, a penalty is deducted from your budget. It is 175 points + 0.5 points for each unit of equipment remaining.

In other words, when the group average equipment is greater than the threshold for that block, no penalty is applied to your budget, regardless of the number of units in your firm’s equipment.
When a Main Check penalty applies, it ranges from 175.5 to 225 points. For example, if your firm's equipment is 50 units, the penalty is: 175 + 0.5 × 50 = 200 points.

In both cases, the block continues to Period 6.

The same conditions apply throughout all blocks in Section 2. A new threshold is determined for each block.

The Main Check at the end of Period 5 follows a different rule in this section.

At the end of Period 5, when all six managers have completed their decisions, the authority observes the group average equipment. Your firm’s equipment is included in this average.

The authority compares the group average equipment with a fixed value called the threshold.

The threshold is fixed at 40 units.

First, the authority checks your firm’s equipment:

  • If your firm’s equipment is 0 (i.e., all units have been retired by Period 5), no penalty is applied.

  • If your firm’s equipment is greater than 0 (i.e., some units remain), whether a penalty is applied depends on the group average equipment.

    • If the group average equipment is greater than the threshold (40 units), no penalty is applied.

    • If the group average equipment is equal to or less than the threshold (40 units), a penalty is deducted from your budget. The penalty is:

      175 points + 0.5 points for each unit of equipment remaining.

In other words, when the group average equipment is greater than the threshold (40 units), no penalty is applied to your budget, regardless of the number of units in your firm’s equipment.
When a Main Check penalty applies, it ranges from 175.5 to 225 points. For example, if your firm's equipment is 50 units, the penalty is: 175 + 0.5 × 50 = 200 points.

The block continues to Period 6.

The Follow-up Check works in the same way as Section 1.

Follow-up Check

After the Main Check, the block continues.

If your firm has equipment after the Main Check, you continue making your decisions.

Follow-up Check

After your decision in Period 10, the authority checks the number of units in your firm’s equipment.

  • If your firm’s equipment is 0 (i.e., all units have been retired by Period 10), no penalty is applied.
  • If your firm’s equipment is greater than 0 (i.e., some units remain by Period 10):
    • 75 points are deducted from your budget as a penalty;
    • your firm’s remaining equipment is automatically retired, and the cost of doing so is also deducted from your budget.

Examples:

1) At the end of Period 10, your firm’s equipment is 0 units, and 800 points are in your budget. No penalty is applied. Your budget therefore remains at 800 points.

2) At the end of Period 10, your firm’s equipment is 25 units, and 800 points are in your budget.

First, 75 points are deducted from your budget as a penalty. The 25 units in your firm’s equipment are then retired. Retiring 25 units costs 62.5 points, so this cost is also deducted.

After the deductions, your budget therefore contains 662.5 points:

800 points − 75 points penalty − 62.5 points retirement cost = 662.5 points.

Your firm’s equipment is therefore 0 at the end of every block.

The automatic retirement cost is 0.10 × remaining equipment². At zero, there is no forced retirement or forced-retirement cost. Your final wallet remains visible in your block summary.

Two starting reports

Before the first decision of this block, please answer these two questions:

  1. At the Main Check at the end of Period 5, how many units of equipment do you expect to have?
  2. At the Main Check at the end of Period 5, how many units of equipment do you expect the group average to be, including yourself in the six-person average?

Submit exactly two whole numbers from 0 to 100. These reports are unincentivised: there is no payment for accuracy, and reports do not directly enter wallet calculations. Both reports remain private; neither report is publicly disclosed.
